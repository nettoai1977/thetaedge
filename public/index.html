Strategy Guide

Double Calendar

Primary strategy - 80% win rate. Sell near-term, buy far-term options.

  • • Entry: VIX 15-20
  • • Strikes: 10% OTM
  • • Take profit: 20-40%
  • • Stop loss: 30% mental

Time Spread

Theta Machine - 300%+ potential. Earn theta daily.

  • • Entry: 20-30 delta
  • • Direction: Any
  • • Risk $162, Reward $618
  • • Exit before expiry

Double Diagonal

Wide range play. Lower Vega risk than double calendar.

  • • Entry: VIX > 20
  • • Use when IV high
  • • Wider profit zone
  • • Lower Vega exposure
