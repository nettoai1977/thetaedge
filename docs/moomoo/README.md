# Moomoo NZ Setup Guide

## Overview

Moomoo is the recommended broker for trading US options from New Zealand. This guide covers account setup, funding, and getting started.

---

## Account Setup

### Step 1: Download the App

- **iOS:** Search "Moomoo" in App Store
- **Android:** Search "Moomoo" in Google Play
- **Web:** https://www.moomoo.com/nz

### Step 2: Sign Up

1. Open the app
2. Tap "Sign Up"
3. Enter your NZ phone number (+64)
4. Verify with SMS code
5. Complete KYC (Know Your Customer):
   - Passport or Driver License
   - Proof of address (utility bill)
   - Tax information (IRD number)

### Step 3: Account Approval

- Usually takes 1-2 business days
- You'll receive email confirmation
- Can start with paper trading immediately

## Funding Your Account

### NZD Deposit (Free!)

**Bank Transfer:**
1. Go to "Assets" → "Deposit"
2. Select "NZD"
3. Copy bank account details
4. Transfer from your NZ bank
5. Funds arrive in 1-2 business days

**No fees for NZD deposits!**

### Convert NZD to USD

**Best Feature: 0 pips spread!**

1. Go to "Assets" → "Currency Exchange"
2. Select NZD → USD
3. Enter amount
4. Confirm exchange
5. USD available immediately

**Example:**
- Deposit: $1,500 NZD
- Exchange rate: ~0.60
- USD received: ~$900 USD
- **Fee: $0!**

## Options Trading Setup

### Step 1: Apply for Options Trading

1. Go to "Trade" → "Options"
2. Complete options questionnaire:
   - Trading experience
   - Income/net worth
   - Investment objectives
3. Wait for approval (usually instant)

### Step 2: Understand the Fee Structure

**Fixed Plan (Recommended for Beginners):**
| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Commission | $0.10/contract |
| Platform Fee | $0.40/contract |
| **Total** | **$0.50/contract** |

**Tiered Plan (For Active Traders):**
| Contracts/Month | Fee/Contract |
|-----------------|--------------|
| 1-50 | $0.55 |
| 51-100 | $0.50 |
| 101-300 | $0.45 |
| 301-500 | $0.40 |
| 501-1,000 | $0.35 |
| 1,001+ | $0.30 |

**Pass-through Fees (per contract):**
- Options Regulatory Fee: $0.013
- OCC Clearing: $0.02
- SEC Fee (sells only): $0.0000206 × amount
- Trading Activity Fee (sells only): $0.00329

### Step 3: Start with Paper Trading

**强烈推荐先用模拟盘！**

1. Go to "Trade" → "Paper Trading"
2. Select "Options"
3. Practice Ravish's strategies
4. Trade for 3-6 months before going live

## Risk Management

### Position Sizing Rules

**With $1,000 USD starting capital:**

| Strategy | Max Contracts | Max Risk |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Double Calendar | 1-2 | $350-700 |
| Time Spread | 1-2 | $160-320 |
| Double Diagonal | 1-2 | $300-600 |

### Stop Loss Rules

**NEVER use physical stop losses on options!**
- Wide bid-ask spreads trigger stops prematurely
- Use mental stops only
- Exit manually when 30% loss reached
